ABOUT THIS REVIEW

Where does marketing add the most value?

Use one score for each activity: 1 = very little contribution, 5 = moderate contribution, 10 = essential contribution. Consider winning and retaining customers, growing market share, supporting salespeople and improving earnings. Supporting work can also have value through the results it enables.

Review 77 tangible activities from the existing programme and current-quarter workplan. Repeated references are combined; broad proposals and longer-term concepts are excluded. The final section contains specific development assignments whose status needs confirmation. No scores are preselected. Leave an activity unrated if you cannot assess it.

A rating records your assessment, not a decision to retain or remove an activity. Costs, commitments, workload and CEO approval must also be considered.
